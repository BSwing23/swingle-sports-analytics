Z4Volleyball Point DetectiveComing Soon
Volleyball Point Detective™

Find out which phase is losing you matches

Every rally will become a fast four-tap sequence: who served, what happened on the serve, did the first attack convert, who won the point. No stat sheets, no film software, no screenshots. Just live taps that build a complete point-by-point record as the match happens.

Most teams know the final score. Few know why. Point Detective will split the match into terminal serving, first ball kill, and transition, then show you which phase is actually deciding your outcomes, rolled up by set, match, and season, so practice time goes toward the real problem instead of a guess. Currently in development — not yet available.

Brian Swingle

Founder & CEO

Brian founded Swingle Sports Analytics after three decades on the volleyball sideline convinced him that coaches needed software built for coaching decisions, not just stat collection. He's coached state kill leaders, called matches as a PA announcer, and manually tracked rotation data on spreadsheets since 2004, the habit that eventually became GASA. His mission is simple: help coaches win more through intelligent analytics.
